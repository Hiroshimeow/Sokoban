# BoxGame

**BoxGame** is a simple yet addictive puzzle game where you need to maneuver boxes into their correct positions by controlling your character.

## How to Play

* **Objective:** Push all the boxes (ice blocks) onto the fire tiles (fire images).
* **Controls:**
    * **A:** Move Left
    * **W:** Move Up
    * **D:** Move Right
    * **S:** Move Down

## Features

* **Puzzle Gameplay:** Challenge your brain with increasingly difficult levels.
* **Multiple Levels:** Play through a variety of stages.
